This Privacy Policy explains how the site collects and uses information. It's written in simple language for clarity.

- Data collected: cookies and similar tracking technologies used to show ads and to analyze traffic.
- Use: to deliver relevant ads, measure site usage, and improve content.
- Third parties: the site may use third-party services (e.g., Google AdSense, analytics providers). These services may set their own cookies.
- Your choices: you can decline ad cookies using the consent banner on the site. Declining may disable personalized ads but does not prevent non-personalized ads in some cases.
- Contact: If you have questions, contact the site owner via the About page.

Please note: This is a minimal starter privacy policy. If your audience includes EU citizens, consult legal advice to ensure GDPR compliance and consider using a detailed consent management platform.
